Designed for passionate readers of Kindle, Kobo, Readmoo, PDF, and physical books. Journalia runs alongside your reading workflow as a floating overlay or desk companion, providing contextual enlightenment, deep thematic analysis, and bidirectional voice discussions without breaking your focus.

🛡️

Anti-Spoiler Engine

AI companion knowledge is dynamically grounded strictly up to your active reading page, preventing premature plot spoilers.

⚡

Local-First Storage

Single source of truth via local Room SQLite. 100% offline available with optional private Google Drive AppData sync.

📷

Ephemeral RAM Capture

Screen and camera images exist only in volatile RAM for OCR and are immediately destroyed. Zero disk saves, zero persistent tracking.

🎙️

Multimodal Voice HUD

Fluid hands-free voice dialogue with custom reading personas, low-latency audio transcription, and immediate audio feedback.

Engineering Ethos

Why We Build Differently

We adhere to three fundamental engineering invariants across every app we release.

🔒 Privacy Sovereignty

We refuse the ad-driven data-harvesting model. Our applications store personal notes and private records on your personal device or personal cloud storage, never on shared commercial databases.

🔋 Ephemeral AI Processing

When our apps leverage computer vision or voice STT, media frames are processed ephemerally in RAM and wiped cleanly right after inference. Your physical book pages and device screen remain private.

🎨 Native Ergonomics

Every interaction is optimized for battery efficiency, minimal latency, and zero distraction. We design tools that fade into the background so you can stay in flow.
