Privacy Policy

Developer: JennyApps • Application: Marginalia AI (com.jennyapps.marginalia) • Effective Date: October 2026

Our Core Privacy Commitment: JennyApps designs applications with a Local-First and Zero-Server-Database architecture. We believe your reading habits, thoughts, and book notes are deeply personal. We do not sell your personal data, build advertising profiles, or store your library on central servers.

1. Information We Process & How We Use It

Marginalia AI is built from the ground up to respect user autonomy and privacy:

  • Local Reading Data: Book titles, authors, current progress, reading milestones, and note excerpts are stored locally on your device via an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database (Room). This data remains on your physical device at all times.
  • Ephemeral Camera & Screen Capture (OCR): When you choose to capture a book page via camera or overlay screenshot, the image is loaded into volatile device memory (RAM) strictly to perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and conversational grounding. As soon as text recognition concludes, the raw image is immediately destroyed in memory (bitmap.recycle()). We never save raw screen captures or camera photos to your disk or upload them to persistent image storage.
  • Voice Interaction & Microphone: Voice Mode is triggered only on demand when you explicitly tap to converse with your reading companion. Audio snippets are streamed ephemerally for transcription (Speech-to-Text) and dialogue reasoning. We do not record or monitor audio in the background.
  • Google Drive Cloud Sync (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud backup, the app connects directly to your personal Google Drive using the restricted drive.appdata scope. Backups reside exclusively inside your private cloud storage folder, inaccessible to third parties and not accessible by JennyApps central servers.

2. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

To deliver advanced contextual reading assistance, Marginalia AI interfaces with trusted cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Gemini API / Google Cloud: Provides text extraction and companion reasoning. Only the extracted text snippet of the current page and your active inquiry are transmitted under strict enterprise privacy terms. Data is not used to train public machine learning models.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app subscriptions and transactions are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers, billing addresses, or financial credentials.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your data is hosted locally on your device:

  • You can clear all reading data and conversation history at any time from within the application's Settings → Storage / Clear Data, or by clearing application storage in Android system settings.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored data.
  • If you synced data to Google Drive, you can delete the backup folder directly from your Google Drive app management settings.
  • If you need assistance regarding data deletion, please contact us at the support address below.

4. Children’s Privacy

Marginalia AI and JennyApps services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards. All external API communications occur over secure, encrypted TLS (HTTPS) connections. Internal services utilize container isolation and minimal privilege permissions.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any modifications will be posted to this website with a revised effective date.
